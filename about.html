The Story

Grooming as
it should be

Shaghaf Gents Salon was founded on a simple belief: that a gentleman's grooming is not an errand, but a ritual. A few quiet moments of care, performed well, that shape the rest of the day.

Our barbers are craftsmen first — precise in their technique, unhurried in their attention, and devoted to the details others overlook. We use premium products, keep conversation easy and appointments unhurried.

Whether you are in for a quick trim or an extended grooming session, you leave Shaghaf composed, polished and a little more yourself.
