What services does Shaghaf Gents Salon offer?

We offer precision haircuts, beard sculpting, hot-towel shaves, hair styling, facials and skin care, hair treatments, manicure & pedicure, kids' cuts and full grooming combination rituals.

Where is Shaghaf Gents Salon located?

We are located in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. Use the map above for directions, or open our Google Maps listing.

How do I book an appointment?

Call us at +971 52 100 1084 or send a message on WhatsApp. The booking form on our contact page also opens in WhatsApp, pre-filled.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome when chairs are available, but we recommend booking in advance — especially on weekends — to guarantee your preferred time.

What products does the salon use?

We use premium grooming and hair-care products selected for quality, and clean, professional tools for every service.
